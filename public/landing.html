What you get

Everything WebPlotDigitizer
should have been

⊕
Guided Calibration
Step-by-step 4-point axis calibration wizard. Drag calibration points after placing them — no need to redo from scratch.
✦
Auto-Trace
Click a line to pick its color. PlotVision uses LAB color matching to trace the entire curve automatically.
📊
Live Preview
See your extracted data rendered as a live chart as you digitize. Spot errors instantly, no export required.
🗂️
Multi-Dataset
Multiple data series on one chart? No problem. Manage unlimited datasets, each with its own color and name.
📐
Measurement Tools
Measure distances, angles, and polygon areas directly on the chart in calibrated data units.
💾
Project Save/Load
Save your entire session — image, calibration, and all data points — as a .pvz file. Resume exactly where you left off.
📄
PDF Support
Open multi-page PDFs directly. Navigate pages and digitize charts from scientific papers without converting first.
↩
Full Undo/Redo
Every action is undoable — point additions, calibration changes, auto-trace commits. 100-step history.
⌨️
Keyboard-First
Every tool, action, and export has a keyboard shortcut. Power users can work entirely without touching the mouse.
