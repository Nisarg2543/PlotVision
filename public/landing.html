What you get

Everything WebPlotDigitizer
should have been

Guided Calibration
Step-by-step 4-point wizard. Drag calibration markers after placing — no need to restart from scratch if you misclick.
Auto-Trace
Click a pixel to pick its color. PlotVision uses perceptual LAB ΔE matching to trace the entire curve automatically, column by column.
Live Preview
See your extracted data rendered as a live chart as you digitize. Spot outliers and errors instantly — no export needed to verify.
Multi-Dataset
Multiple series on one chart? Manage unlimited datasets with individual colors, visibility toggles, and sorting controls.
Measurement Tools
Measure distances, angles, and polygon areas directly on the canvas in calibrated data-space units.
Project Save / Load
Save your entire session — image, calibration, and all data — as a compact .pvz file. Resume exactly where you left off.
PDF Support
Open multi-page PDFs directly. Navigate pages and digitize charts from scientific papers without converting to images first.
Full Undo / Redo
Every action is undoable — point additions, calibration changes, auto-trace commits. 100-step history, always available.
Keyboard-First
Every tool, action, and export has a shortcut. Power users can work entirely without a mouse — from open to export.
